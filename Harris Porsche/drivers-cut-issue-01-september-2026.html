Here’s what’s news.

On the Road

2027 911 Carrera S MT · Porsche

America keeps the third pedal

Here is the honest 911 this fall, and it is not a hundred-car Alpine special you will read about and never see. It is a 2027 Carrera S with a six-speed manual bolted into the middle of the cabin, and Porsche is selling it here.

Rear-wheel drive only. The 3.0-liter twin-turbo flat-six, 473 horsepower, and three pedals under your feet — no hybrid weight, no paddle shuffle. It is a North America exclusive, which almost never runs in our favor and this time does.

The GT3 Touring take rate on the stick ran eighty percent. That is not nostalgia. That is a market telling the factory what it actually wants, and the S got a manual because of it. Porsche’s line, roughly: they’ll keep building the third pedal until the law or the order book kills it. The hybrid GTS and Turbo still don’t offer one. That tells you which car in the range is the driver’s car.

On the Table

Elsie’s Plate & Pie · Mid City

Named for Elsie

Paul Chauvin Dupré named the place for his grandmother. Elsie Marie Campeau Rupe, out of Lockport, down in Lafourche where the water gets into everything — the cooking, the names, the way people talk about a meal three days after they ate it. That is the whole thesis of Elsie’s Plate & Pie.

Order like you mean it. The seafood pot pie, because a pot pie built by someone from bayou country is a different animal than the frozen-aisle memory you’re carrying. The seafood Lafourche bisque. And a slice of pie, boxed, to take home — the move of a person who plans ahead and regrets nothing. Two people. No laptops.

OpenTable will not book you here. Call the house — (225) 636-5157 — or start at the site. Old-fashioned, and it works.

BoDeans, September 16

On the Wrist

Bathyscaphe 70th · ref. 5903 1110 63A

Blancpain goes small on purpose

The Fifty Fathoms is where the modern dive watch starts — 1953, the actual origin, not a marketing claim. For the 70th, Blancpain didn’t chase size. It shrank the Bathyscaphe to 37.4 mm and built 300 of them. The restraint is the point.

Steel, 37.4 mm, 12.3 mm thick, 46.5 mm lug to lug. Sapphire front and back, ceramic bezel, 300 meters. Sunburst meteor grey dial, vintage Blancpain logo, 18k white gold baton hands, vintage-toned Super-LumiNova. It reads like 1968 without cosplaying it.

Turn it over: manufacture caliber 1150, 100-hour reserve, silicon hairspring, 3 Hz, open-worked 18k gold rotor from the ’53 Fifty Fathoms. Chocolate calf. $18,300. Available now. Three hundred people get one.

On the Road

Mission S, above the 911

Porsche Mission S · Queens, New York

Mission S, above the 911

Porsche is planning a permanent mid-engine super sports car line above the 911. The Mission S concept is the preview.

Porsche showed it on October 15 on a pier in Queens, New York, the same pier where the 550 Spyder was photographed in 1953. The cockpit mixes analog and digital controls. Porsche told Car and Driver the line will take gasoline, hybrid and plug-in hybrid power. Porsche says the line will stay combustion and hybrid.

No price. No date. Car and Driver doesn’t expect it before 2029. Porsche’s October 7 strategy, Sportwagenschmiede ’35, cuts model variants by about 20 percent and adds more emotive 911 derivatives.

On the Table

Where Sammy’s Grill used to be

Historic Highland Road Grill · 8683 Highland Road

Where Sammy’s Grill used to be

For nearly 40 years, 8683 Highland Road was Sammy’s Grill. Willie Chapman of Willie’s Restaurant on Coursey Boulevard took the building in July. He soft-opened the Historic Highland Road Grill on October 15, and about 30 percent of the menu pays homage to Sammy’s.

Order the 2 Links: boudin, smoked sausage, a goat cheese ball, cheese and house pickles. Then the gumbo fries, which come from Willie’s. The Prime Rib Press is another nod to Sammy’s: chimichurri, onion ragu, mozzarella, au jus. The drink is the Historic Storm, light and dark rum with passion fruit syrup and lemon.

Now open Thursday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 10 p.m., and Sunday, 11 a.m. to 9 p.m. (225) 408-3028.

In the Cup

Vintage chairs on Government Street

Las Brujas Coffee & Grocery · 3101 Government Street, Mid City

Vintage chairs on Government Street

Las Brujas sits on Government Street in Mid City, the neighborhood where David Villa grew up. He named it for the farm in Envigado, Colombia, where four generations of his family lived. He was born in the farmhouse, as were his grandfather and aunt.

Vintage chairs, a lamp and plants make a warm corner; a sofa and lamps make the lounge. The beans are air-roasted. Villa wanted coffee and a grocery, “a little bodega with fresh baked bread, milk and eggs,” he told The Reveille, which called it a third space. Opening day, it poured more than 200 drinks in three hours.

Tuesday to Friday, 6 a.m. to 4 p.m., and weekends, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m., closed Monday. Text (225) 444-5735.

On the Wrist

Datograph Perpetual, on platinum

A. Lange & Söhne Datograph Perpetual · ref. 410.136E

Datograph Perpetual, on platinum

The Datograph Perpetual came out in 2006. It was the first Lange to put a chronograph, a perpetual calendar and an outsize date in one wristwatch. For the 2026 version, released October 24, Lange made the case and the bracelet in 950 platinum. It is limited to 50 pieces.

41 mm across, 13.5 mm thick. Black dial, eleven diamond hour markers. A five-row platinum bracelet with a deployant buckle. Inside is the hand-wound L952.1: 556 parts, 36 hours of reserve.

Lange lists it as price on request. Hodinkee’s hands-on puts it at $276,000.

Range and charge

More miles. Less time plugged in.

Tesla’s volume Model Y is rated up to 321 miles. The iX3 is 383 miles on standard 20" all-seasons and 434 miles on the no-cost 20" summers. At an 800-volt station the iX3 adds about 185 miles in 10 minutes. Tesla quotes up to 172 miles in 15 minutes on Model Y L. If the brief is highway range and a short stop, this is not close.

The drive

A Sports Activity Vehicle. Not a tablet with wheels.

Tesla Performance is quicker to 60. That is fair. The iX3 is the one built as a BMW SAV: dual-motor xDrive, 463 hp in Sport, Heart of Joy coordinating drive, brake, and recuperation. Panoramic iDrive keeps information on the road, not only on a center screen. If you still want to enjoy the drive, that is the split.

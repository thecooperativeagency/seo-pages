Brakes

Brakes

Pads wear down. If metal hits the rotor, damage gets expensive fast and stopping power drops.

  • Common signs: squeal, grind, longer stops, vibration in the pedal
  • CBS tracks front and rear pad status
  • Genuine BMW pads, rotors, and sensors are stocked for complete brake service
Schedule Brake Check

Pad wear

Do not wait for grind

When pad material is gone, the backing plate can score the rotor. That turns a pad job into pads and rotors.

If you hear grind, smell hot brakes, or feel pull/vibration, bring it in. Brake fluid is a separate time-based service on the CBS display.

Tires

Tires

Tread is your contact patch. When it is gone, wet roads and hard stops get worse quickly.

  • Wear bars are molded into the tread. When the tread is even with the bars, you are at about 2/32 inch — replace the tire
  • Penny test: if you can see all of Lincoln’s head, tread is too low
  • Uneven wear often points to alignment or inflation issues
  • Premium fitments commonly used on BMWs: Michelin, Pirelli, Continental, Bridgestone, Goodyear
Book Tire Check

On the rack

Stocked at the store

BMW of Jackson keeps tire inventory in the service bay so the right fitment is ready when you need it.

Look across the tread for raised wear bars. When the rubber is even with the bars, you are at about 2/32 inch. Do not wait for cords.

Battery

Battery

Modern BMWs put heavy demand on the electrical system. A weak battery can look like a bigger electronic problem.

  • Have the battery tested at service visits — especially before summer heat and winter cold
  • Short trips, heat, and sitting unused all stress batteries
  • Replacement often needs coding/registration so charge management stays correct
Schedule Battery Test

Original BMW batteries

Designed for your model

Genuine BMW batteries are engineered for the power draw of today’s connected BMWs and the integration your car expects.

If the car sits, takes short trips only, or struggles to start, put a battery test on the next visit.

Cabin microfilter

Cabin air filter

Outside air enters through the HVAC system. The cabin microfilter catches dust, pollen, and debris before that air hits the cabin.

  • Over time the filter loads up with dirt and can hold odor
  • Symptoms: weak airflow, musty smell, more allergens in the cabin
  • BMW often combines cabin microfilter service with specific engine-oil service occurrences
Replace Cabin Filter

What happens over time

Dust, pollen, odor

The filter loads up. Airflow drops. A/C can start to smell. People with allergies notice first.

This is one of the highest-value quick services on a BMW and is commonly done with oil service when CBS/combined items call for it.

Should I lease, finance, or buy a BMW in Mississippi and in Jackson, Mississippi?

Lease if you want a newer BMW every few years on a set number of miles. Finance if you want to own it, drive as much as you like, and keep it past the factory warranty. Consumer Reports says buying usually wins if you will keep the car six years or more.

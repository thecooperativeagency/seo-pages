Vehicle service contract — 84 months or 100,000 miles

Extra repair coverage after the factory warranty. If you are financing, this is the plan we strongly suggest, because a loan often lasts past 4 years or 50,000 miles. On a short lease it is usually a lower priority. You can buy it either way. Ask for the price in writing.

Gap protection

If the car is totaled or stolen, gap protection can cover the difference between the insurance check and what you still owe. You can buy it here. On many BMW Financial Services leases it is already included — we will check your contract. On a loan, it is optional, and worth talking about if you put little money down or take a long term.

Questions

Common questions

Can a dealer make me buy gap protection or extra repair coverage?

No. The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau and the Federal Trade Commission say these products are optional and not required to get a loan. Ask for prices in writing.

If I am financing, what would you suggest?

We strongly suggest a vehicle service contract — extra repair coverage for 84 months or 100,000 miles — because a loan often lasts past BMW’s 4-year/50,000-mile factory warranty. Year five is on you unless you add a plan. You can still buy the other plans. Here is what you can buy, and here is what we would suggest.

If I am leasing, what would you suggest?

We highlight tire and wheel protection and key replacement. Those are the items that show up at turn-in — a cut tire, a gouged wheel, a lost key. You can still buy the other plans. Nothing is required.

Do I need gap protection on a BMW lease?

Gap protection can cover the difference between the insurance check and what you still owe after a total loss. You can buy it here. On many BMW Financial Services leases it is already included — we will check your contract. On a loan, it is optional.

Is extra repair coverage worth it on a short lease?

A typical lease stays inside the factory 4-year/50,000-mile warranty, so a vehicle service contract is usually a lower priority on a short lease. If you are financing and will keep the car past year four, that is when we would suggest it.

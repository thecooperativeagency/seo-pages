On Saturday

LSU 2–0 walks into Oxford

Two games, two statements. Clemson left 51–10. Louisiana Tech left 45–14. Lane Kiffin’s first LSU September looks loud on paper. The paper gets heavier this weekend.

Saturday, September 19, the Tigers go to Oxford to play No. 8 Ole Miss — 7:30 p.m. CT, the first real weight of the year. Get through that and No. 9 Texas A&M comes to Tiger Stadium on September 26, also 7:30, with rivalry teeth in it. Two ranked opponents in eight days. McNeese follows at home October 3 at 7:45.

Southern University

Mumford, 7:00 p.m. Next week they go to Jackson State. Homecoming is October 3 against Arkansas–Pine Bluff. LSU owns the national TV. Southern owns the neighborhood.

Five-dollar tacos land on Perkins

The pink building on Perkins used to be Rock Paper Taco. Now it is Vals — New Orleans taco house, first Baton Rouge room, horseshoe bar, $5 tacos on the menu, margaritas that are not an afterthought. The partners also run Cure in New Orleans. Happy hour 4 to 6. Casual, loud enough, no dress code.

Teddy Graham Latte at Light House

Same city, different errand than dinner. Light House on Lee: specialty coffee, own roast, a room that hires with intention.

The short answer

Lease if you want a newer BMW on a clock. Finance if you want to own it.

A typical lease stays inside the 4-year/50,000-mile factory warranty and included scheduled maintenance for 3 years or 36,000 miles. Finance if you want your name on the title, no mileage cap, and you are comfortable owning the car after the factory warranty. Protection products are optional.

Lease if you…

A newer BMW for a set time and miles.

You pay for the years you drive, not the whole car.

  • A set term and mileage
  • Your contract sets the miles
  • Usually still inside the factory warranty and included maintenance
  • Comfortable with an inspection when you return it
  • Often a lower monthly payment than a similar loan — BMW’s framing, not a quote from this store
Finance if you…

Your name on the title.

No mileage cap. No inspection when you are done. You can personalize the car.

  • You own it
  • No extra wear or mile bills at the end
  • BMW Financial Services lists terms up to 72 months
  • Past year four, a vehicle service contract is worth talking about

Louisiana tax

You pay tax on the payments, not the whole car.

State sales tax is 5%. In Louisiana, the City of Baton Rouge / East Baton Rouge local rate as of July 1, 2026 is 5.50% plus 5% state — 10.50% combined. Vehicle tax follows where the car lives. We are not claiming extra economic-development-district rates apply to vehicles until that is confirmed.

Questions

FAQ

Should I lease or finance a BMW in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

Lease if you want a newer BMW for a set time and miles, usually still inside the 4-year/50,000-mile factory warranty. Finance if you want your name on the title, no mileage cap, and no inspection when you are done. Consumer Reports’ rule of thumb: if you plan to keep a car six years or more, buying is almost always the better money.

How many miles can I drive on a BMW lease?

Your contract is the number that counts. A higher yearly limit costs more each month. Some customers can buy extra miles later. Ask us to show the miles on your deal.

Who handles BMW leases and loans?

Leases and many purchase loans run through BMW Financial Services. You can manage an account in the My BMW app or at BMW Financial Services My Account.

How is a BMW lease taxed in Louisiana?

Louisiana taxes the lease payments, not the full price of the car. State sales tax is 5%. Local rates depend on where the car lives. The leasing company may also be billed a personal property tax, which can be added to your payment.

Can I buy my leased BMW?

Yes, at any time. Ask for a payoff quote through BMW Financial Services or the My BMW app. If you buy it, you are not billed for extra wear or extra miles at turn-in.

What happens when the lease ends?

You can trade up to a new BMW, buy the car, or return it. BMW Financial Services offers a complimentary pre-inspection. The estimate shows up in My BMW.

How it works

You do not pay sales tax on the whole sticker when you lease.

Louisiana taxes the lease payments. When you buy, the Office of Motor Vehicles collects tax on the price minus trade-in and rebate, based on where the car lives. We will run the tax on your deal.

Title and plates

The paperwork is the same either way.

Title $68.50. Lien $15 or $10. Handling is $8. Local fee up to $6. Plates are based on vehicle value, sold two years at a time.

Questions

FAQ

Do you pay sales tax on a lease in Louisiana?

Yes. Louisiana taxes the lease payments, not the sticker price. We will run the tax on your deal.

How much are title and plates in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

Title is $68.50. Recording a lien is $15 or $10. Handling is $8. Local fee up to $6. Passenger plates are based on vehicle value, sold two years at a time ($20 minimum). Vehicle tax is based on where the car lives.

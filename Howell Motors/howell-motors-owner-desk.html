OEM co-op — what these two brands actually allow

Exact Howell balances and match rates live in the dealer portals. Outside sources do not publish this rooftop’s accrual. What is public:

GMC — GM In-Market Retail (IMR) / Dealer Digital Solution

  • GM connects Chevy / Buick / GMC / Cadillac dealers to certified digital partners. Channels called out by approved vendors: paid media, SEO, social media management, paid social.
  • Industry guides put GM IMR reimbursement in a 50–100% range by channel, typically quarterly, use-it-or-lose-it. Verify on GM SharePoint / IMR account — do not quote 50% or 100% as Howell’s rate.
  • Digital Dealer (Naked Lime approval): more than 90% of GM’s ~4,300 U.S. dealers participate; GM reimburses a portion of eligible costs from approved vendors.
  • Enrollment / dashboard: gmdealerdigital.com · dealer.digital@gm.com · 1-800-250-9038.
  • SEO is eligible when billed by a certified DAP. Organic posting on Facebook is generally not an ad spend and is not reimbursable.

Nissan — Dealer Digital Program (not a DIY Google Ads invoice)

  • Nissan runs a certified marketplace (nissandigitalprogram.com). Dealer Inspire is on the Full-Suite advertising list — which matches their website vendor.
  • 2026 DI advertising sheet: $2,500 minimum media + 25% management (cap $3,500). That is the on-menu paid path, not a Coop-invented number.
  • A3 Brands (agency, not OEM): they report helping Nissan dealers apply co-op to SEO; they also say many OEMs reimburse 30–50% of SEO. Treat as vendor marketing, not Nissan policy.
  • A 2018–2020 Nissan Northwest Region PDF (not Mississippi, not current): dealers accrued 0.5% of wholesale, Nissan added $0.40 per $1, claims 1:1, SEM/SEO required invoices + keyword lists + screenshots, 60-day claim window. Useful as “this is how Nissan thinks,” not as Summit’s 2026 contract.
Hard line for later The Cooperative Agency is not listed as a GM IMR DAP or a Nissan Digital Program partner on the public partner boards. Do not promise that Coop invoices will reimburse.
On co-op in this call We can call GMC dealer digital and Nissan Digital Program with you (or for you) and ask what SEO is claimable — same motion we use with BMW. GMC/Nissan are not BMW; they pay through their certified lists. We will not say a Cooperative invoice comes back until the OEM says it does.

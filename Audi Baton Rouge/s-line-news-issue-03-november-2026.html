On the Road

The Audi SQ9: Three Rows, Twin-Turbo V8

On the Road

The Audi SQ9: Three Rows, Twin-Turbo V8

Last issue was the Q9. This is the one with the V8. The SQ9 runs a 4.0-liter twin-turbo with 591 horsepower and 590 lb-ft. Audi quotes 0 to 60 mph in 3.8 seconds and calls it best in class. Three rows, available second-row captain's chairs. From $118,000 before destination. U.S. deliveries begin in the fourth quarter of 2026.

The quattro all-wheel-drive system is rear-biased, with an electronically controlled rear differential lock and staggered tires on 22-inch wheels, 23s optional. Rear-wheel steering is standard. So are Digital Matrix LED headlights with adaptive driving beam, a first for the U.S. market.

Photos: Audi of America. Sources: Audi of America, 28 Jul 2026; Road & Track, 28 Oct 2026. Price before destination.

Mansurs, 30-Plus Years on Corporate

Mansurs on the Boulevard · 5720-A Corporate Blvd.

On the Table

Mansurs, 30-Plus Years on Corporate

Mansurs on the Boulevard has been on Corporate for more than 30 years. A mahogany bar, a pianist in the dining room at night. The order is the Eggplant Camellia: lightly fried eggplant medallions, jumbo lump crabmeat, dill hollandaise.

Add the Chicken, Duck and Andouille Gumbo, or ask for All That Jazz, a demitasse of each of the three soups. Then the Cedar-Roasted Redfish, the dish the house says made it famous. Business casual. Call for a table.

In the Cup

Le Rêve, Beans From Lafayette

Le Rêve opened April 20 in Southdowns Village. The beans come from Rêve Coffee Roasters in Lafayette, Louisiana. The drink is the Honey Mocha: espresso, mocha, and honey with oat milk. Hot in 12 or 16 ounces, iced in 16 or 24.

Order ahead and pick up at the drive-thru window. Opens at 6 a.m.

On the Wrist

The Hermès H08 in Bleu de Malte

The new H08 Bleu de Malte has a 39 × 39 mm cushion case in braided glass-fiber composite, without slate dust. Blue ceramic accents. A crystal-sandblasted rhodium dial. Orange details.

Inside is the H1837 automatic movement from Vaucher Manufacture Fleurier. On rubber. $8,600. Not limited. A white Blanc Chantilly version sits beside it at the same price.

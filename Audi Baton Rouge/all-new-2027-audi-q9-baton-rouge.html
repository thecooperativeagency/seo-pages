New flagship

The first Audi Q9

The all-new 2027 Audi Q9 is Audi’s largest SUV yet — a full-size three-row flagship with a long wheelbase, business-class second-row options, and the brand’s biggest panoramic sunroof. It is built for owners who want space without giving up modern Audi tech.

Space & comfort

Room for seven. Calm for all of them.

Seven seats are standard. Available power-adjustable second-row captain’s chairs create a six-seat layout with a more private second row. The standard panoramic sunroof is the largest Audi has fitted, with optional switchable transparency.

  • Standard 7-passenger seating
  • Available 2nd-row captain’s chairs
  • Largest panoramic sunroof ever on an Audi
  • Up to 87.7 cu-ft cargo with rows folded
Lighting & arrival

Curved OLED. Automatic doors.

Available third-generation curved digital OLED taillights are a production first, flowing with the body and widening the light signature. Available automatic doors open and close on command, with obstacle detection to help protect people and objects nearby.

  • Available curved digital OLED taillights
  • Available Digital Matrix LED headlights
  • Available automatic doors with soft close
  • Available advanced turn-signal projection
Power & control

Flagship presence. Twin-turbo V6.

A 2.9-liter twin-turbo V6 delivers 429 horsepower and 442 lb-ft, with 0–60 in 4.9 seconds. Standard quattro all-wheel drive and an 8-speed Tiptronic keep the Q9 composed whether you are loaded with people, cargo, or both.

  • 2.9L twin-turbo V6 · 429 hp / 442 lb-ft
  • 0–60 mph in 4.9 seconds
  • Standard quattro® all-wheel drive
  • Composed full-size SUV dynamics

Flagship details worth a long look

Exterior stance, OLED lighting, panoramic glass, and a fully digital cabin.

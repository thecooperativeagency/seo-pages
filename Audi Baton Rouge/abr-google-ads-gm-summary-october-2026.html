What we did

  • Built Search so Brand, Service, monthly offers, and each model line each have a job.
  • Every month, ads are rewritten from the live specials page — not leftover copy.
  • Stopped paying for junk (competitor / “luxury SUV” research) and stopped service-coupon searches from eating the sales budget.
  • Added a Service Offers campaign from the service-specials page.
  • Turned on Vehicle ads so cars on the lot can show as Google vehicle listings.
  • Built Pre-Owned Q5 Search + Display to the used Q5 page.
  • Turned on AI matching on used Q5 Search, with the landing page locked.

Why

  • Spend on people looking for this store, these cars, and these offers — not random research.
  • What Google shows has to match what the website says.
  • Vehicle ads put actual lot inventory in Google results (including AI surfaces).
  • Used Q5 is metal we need to move.

AI results

They were already showing. There is no separate “AI ads” campaign to buy. Google AI Overviews and AI Mode pull from Search ads and Vehicle ads. Audi Baton Rouge already had both running, so those ads were already eligible.

Why more of them will show now. We turned on AI matching on used Q5 Search so those ads can appear on longer, conversational questions people type into AI search. Google still sends the click to the used Q5 page — we did not let it pick a different URL. Vehicle ads keep feeding inventory cards. We did not turn this on for offer campaigns, because Google would start writing dollar claims and sending people to the wrong page.

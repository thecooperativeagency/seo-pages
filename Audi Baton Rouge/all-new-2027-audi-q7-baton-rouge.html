Redesigned from the ground up

Starting at $70,900, the 2027 Q7 is a complete redesign — more powerful and more capable than the generation it replaces. It seats seven, reaches 60 mph in 4.8 seconds, and tows up to 7,700 pounds when properly equipped.

Performance

Move with intention

A twin-turbo V6 delivers 429 horsepower through quattro® all-wheel drive, reaching 60 mph in 4.8 seconds and pulling up to 7,700 pounds when the situation calls for it.

  • 429 hp twin-turbo V6
  • 0–60 mph in 4.8 seconds
  • Tows up to 7,700 lb when properly equipped
  • quattro® all-wheel drive standard
Space

Seven seats. Room for everything else.

The Q7 seats seven passengers as standard, with available captain’s chairs in the second row for a more relaxed arrangement. Maximum cargo space reaches 78.1 cubic feet.

  • Seven seats standard
  • Available second-row captain’s chairs
  • Up to 78.1 cu-ft cargo space
  • Flexible second- and third-row layout
Design & light

All-new, inside and out

The 2027 Q7 is a full exterior and interior redesign. Available Digital Matrix LED headlights define it at night with adaptive, high-resolution illumination.

  • Completely redesigned exterior
  • Refined all-new interior
  • Available Digital Matrix LED headlights
  • Available digital OLED taillights

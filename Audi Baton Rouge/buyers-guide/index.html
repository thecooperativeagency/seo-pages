Should I lease, finance, or buy an Audi in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

Lease if you like driving a newer Audi every few years. Finance if you want it to be yours, with no mileage cap. The factory warranty, included maintenance, and Louisiana tax all play a part, and every protection product is optional.

Included maintenance

Your lease comes with its maintenance covered

Every new 2026 or newer Audi comes with Audi Signature Care, which covers your scheduled maintenance visits for the first 3 years or 30,000 miles, whichever comes first, at any authorized Audi dealer. On a 3-year lease within 30,000 miles, your routine service is taken care of for the whole lease. If you buy and plan to keep your Audi longer, ask us about Audi Care Select to extend your maintenance coverage.

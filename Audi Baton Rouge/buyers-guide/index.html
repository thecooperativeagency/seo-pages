Should I lease, finance, or buy an Audi?

If you plan to keep the car six years or more, buying is almost always the winning choice — that is Consumer Reports’ rule, not a monthly-payment quote. Luxury captives often discount leases; Audi’s own page still says a lease may offer lower monthly payments on average than financing over a similar term. Your miles, your hang-onto-it horizon, and Louisiana tax on lease payments vs purchase price decide the rest.

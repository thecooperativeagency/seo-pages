Audi Baton Rouge · Buyer’s guide

Where Certified Pre-Owned sits next to a new lease

A shorter warranty stack, a real inspection claim, and a different monthly conversation.

Audi CPO Limited Warranty: 1 year or 20,000 miles, starting after the new-vehicle limited warranty expires (or at purchase if that warranty has already expired). Eligibility: model years 5 or newer, no more than 60,000 miles; 125+ components inspected. HV battery is not treated as CPO-covered on the buyer-journey fact list — factory HV coverage is separate.

CPO is the buyout / used alternative when a new lease isn’t the move. CPO financing and CPO as a lease-return supply both show up in Audi’s own stack. Live inventory: certified-pre-owned collection · program: CPO program.

How Louisiana taxes an Audi lease vs a purchase

Gross proceeds vs domicile purchase tax — with LDR and OMV, not a national residual myth.

Louisiana state sales and use tax is 5%. Leases are taxed on gross proceeds (the payments), not the sticker. Purchases are taxed at the vehicle’s domicile. City of Baton Rouge / East Baton Rouge: 5.50% local + 5.00% state = 10.50% as of July 1, 2026.

Leases vs purchases

LDR: tax base for leases or rentals is gross proceeds. Motor vehicle lease and rental tax is dedicated to the Transportation Trust Fund (R.S. 48:77). Automobile rental tax of 3% applies to rentals of 29 days or less — that is not a lease.

OMV collects sales tax on purchases based on domicile, on price minus trade-in and rebate.

Title and plates

Title $68.50; lien $15 UCC-1 or $10; handling $8; local fee up to $6. Passenger plate: 0.1% of value per year, $10,000 minimum base, 2-year increments ($20 minimum for 2 years).

Questions

FAQ

Do you pay sales tax on a lease in Louisiana?

Yes. Louisiana taxes leases on gross proceeds (the lease payments), not the full vehicle price. State sales and use tax is 5%. Source: Louisiana Department of Revenue.

How much is tax, title and license in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

OMV title $68.50; lien recordation $15 (UCC-1) or $10; handling $8; local fee up to $6. Passenger plate is 0.1% of vehicle value per year with a $10,000 minimum base, sold in 2-year increments ($20 minimum for 2 years). City of Baton Rouge / East Baton Rouge local sales tax is 5.50% plus 5.00% state = 10.50% as of July 1, 2026. Vehicle sales are taxed at domicile — whether economic-development-district add-ons apply to vehicles is not confirmed here.

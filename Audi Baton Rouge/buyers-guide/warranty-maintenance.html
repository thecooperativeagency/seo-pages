What does the Audi warranty actually cover?

The clocks that make a lease feel “included” — and the years a financed owner still has to plan for.

4 years or 50,000 miles new-vehicle limited warranty. 12-year corrosion. 4-year roadside. MY2026+ Signature Care covers scheduled maintenance for the first 3 years or 30,000 miles. A typical lease lives inside that bubble; a long finance term does not.

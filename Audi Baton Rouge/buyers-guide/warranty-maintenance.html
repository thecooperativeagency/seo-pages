FAQ

What does the Audi warranty cover, and for how long?

The new-car limited warranty is 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first. Roadside help is 4 years, unlimited miles. Rust-through is 12 years. Plug-in hybrid batteries are 8 years or 100,000 miles.

Does Audi have a separate powertrain warranty?

The pages we checked fold powertrain into the 4-year/50,000-mile new-car warranty. Longer powertrain coverage is sold as an optional service plan.

Is Audi maintenance included?

On 2026 and newer models, scheduled maintenance is included for the first 3 years or 30,000 miles (Audi Signature Care). Older model years can add prepaid Audi Care.

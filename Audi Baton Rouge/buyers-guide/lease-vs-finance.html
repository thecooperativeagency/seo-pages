The short answer

Lease if you like a newer Audi every few years. Finance if you want to make it yours.

A typical lease stays inside the 4-year/50,000-mile factory warranty and, on 2026 and newer cars, included scheduled maintenance for 3 years or 30,000 miles. Finance if you want your name on the title, no mileage cap, and you’re comfortable owning the car after the factory warranty. Every protection product is optional.

Lease if you…

A newer Audi for a set time and miles.

You pay for the years you drive, not the whole car.

  • A set term and mileage
  • Usually 15,000 miles a year or fewer
  • Still inside the factory warranty and included maintenance
  • Comfortable with an inspection when you return it
  • Often a lower monthly payment than a similar loan (we’ll quote your actual numbers)
Finance if you…

Your name on the title.

No mileage cap. No inspection when you’re done. Make it yours however you like.

  • You own it
  • No extra wear or mile bills at the end
  • Terms from 12 to 72 months
  • Purchase loans from May 1, 2025 run through Audi Retail Finance, powered by Wells Fargo
  • Past year four, an extra service plan is worth talking about

Louisiana tax

You pay tax on the payments, not the whole car.

State sales tax is 5%. In Louisiana, the City of Baton Rouge / East Baton Rouge local rate as of July 1, 2026 is 5.50% plus 5% state — 10.50% combined. Vehicle tax follows where the car lives. If you live in a special tax district, ask us and we’ll confirm whether any extra rate applies to your car.

There’s no wrong answer, just the one that fits how you drive. Here’s how to tell which is right for you in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge.

Lease if you want a newer Audi for a set time and miles, usually still inside the 4-year/50,000-mile factory warranty. Finance if you want your name on the title, no mileage cap, and no inspection when you’re done. Consumer Reports’ rule of thumb: if you plan to keep a car six years or more, buying is almost always the better money.

How many miles can I drive on an Audi lease?

Most Audi leases allow 15,000 miles a year or fewer. A higher limit costs more each month. Some customers can buy extra miles at the halfway point. Your contract has the final number.

Who handles Audi leases and loans now?

Leases are with Audi Financial Services. Purchase loans started on or after May 1, 2025 go through Audi Retail Finance, powered by Wells Fargo. Audi Financial Services doesn’t refinance.

How is an Audi lease taxed in Louisiana?

Louisiana taxes the lease payments, not the full price of the car. State sales tax is 5%. Local rates depend on where the car lives. The leasing company may also be billed a personal property tax, which can be added to your payment.

Can I buy my leased Audi?

Yes, at any time. Ask for a payoff quote in the myAudi app. If you buy it, you skip the turn-in fee and you won’t be billed for extra wear or extra miles.

What happens when the lease ends?

You can trade up to a new Audi, extend, buy the car, or return it. Audi sends an independent inspector to look at the car before you return it.

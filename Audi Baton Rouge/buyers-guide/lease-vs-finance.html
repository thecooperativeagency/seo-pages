Should I lease or finance an Audi in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

A brand-specific answer: warranty clocks, Audi Care, miles, and Louisiana tax on payments vs purchase — with Audi USA and LDR cited.

Lease if you want a newer Audi for a set time and miles, usually still inside the 4-year/50,000-mile new-vehicle limited warranty and Audi Signature Care on MY2026+. Finance if you want the title, no mileage cap, and you are comfortable owning past the factory warranty. Protection products are optional.

This page is for buying or leasing an Audi in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge. It uses Audi USA and Audi Financial Services language, then Louisiana tax — not a national “most states don’t tax residual” line that does not fit here.

Decision

Lease your Audi if you… / Finance your Audi if you…

Lease if you…

  • Want a new Audi for a set amount of time and miles (Audi USA).
  • Drive a schedule that fits typically 15,000 or fewer miles per year.
  • Want the term to sit inside 4 yr/50k warranty, 4-year roadside, and Signature Care (first 3 yr/30k on MY2026+).
  • Are comfortable with a lease-end inspection by Audi’s third-party inspector (AiM) and the wear rules.
  • Like lower payments on average vs financing a similar term — Audi’s own framing, not a quote.

Finance if you…

  • Want your name on the title (Audi USA).
  • Want no end-of-lease excess wear, mileage, or turn-in math.
  • Want no mileage restrictions and freedom to personalize.
  • Will keep the car after the 4/50 warranty — where a VSC or CPO-style coverage enters the chat.
  • Want terms from 12 to 72 months (Audi Retail Finance powered by Wells Fargo).

How is this taxed in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

State sales and use tax is 5%. Leases are taxed on gross proceeds — the payments — not the full vehicle price. Purchases are taxed by OMV based on where the vehicle is domiciled, on price minus trade-in and rebate. City of Baton Rouge / East Baton Rouge local rate as of July 1, 2026 is 5.50% local + 5.00% state = 10.50%. Baker and Central are listed at 11.00%. Whether economic-development-district add-ons apply to vehicle sales is not confirmed on this page.

Audi Financial Services lists Louisiana among states where local jurisdictions may send AFS a personal property tax bill on leased vehicles; that tax is added to the lease balance.

Warranty, Care, and why the clock matters

New Vehicle Limited Warranty: 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever first, starting when the vehicle is delivered to the original purchaser or original lessee. Corrosion perforation: 12 years, regardless of mileage. PHEV high-voltage battery: 8 years or 100,000 miles. Roadside: 4 years unlimited miles on new vehicles (the coverage is for the vehicle, not the person).

A lease that ends before those clocks usually never sees an uncovered repair. A financed owner who keeps the car into year five is in a different conversation — Vehicle Service Protection, Care Select, or CPO if they buy used. Details: warranty and maintenance.

Lease-end, buyout, CPO

Turn in, extend, purchase, or return. Buy the car and you skip turn-in fee, excess wear, and excess miles. CPO is a separate path: 1 year or 20,000 miles after the NVLW (or at purchase if NVLW is already done). Lease-end guide · CPO · Trade-in and buyout.

Questions

FAQ

Should I lease or finance an Audi in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

Lease if you want a newer Audi on a set term and mileage, typically inside the 4-year/50,000-mile new-vehicle limited warranty. Finance if you want your name on the title, no mileage cap, and no lease-end wear review. Audi USA’s own EV lease-or-finance page uses the same split. Keep-it-six-years-or-more is Consumer Reports’ buy rule, not an Audi payment quote.

How many miles can I drive on an Audi lease?

Audi Financial Services says standard leases are typically 15,000 or fewer miles per year. A higher limit means a higher payment. Eligible customers can buy additional miles at the halfway point. Your contract is the number that counts.

Who finances Audi now — Audi Financial Services or Wells Fargo?

Leases and finance contracts started before May 1, 2025 are serviced by Audi Financial Services. Finance contracts started on or after May 1, 2025 are Audi Retail Finance powered by Wells Fargo. AFS does not refinance vehicles at this time.

How is an Audi lease taxed in Louisiana?

Louisiana taxes leases on gross proceeds — the lease payments — not the full vehicle price (Louisiana Department of Revenue). State sales and use tax is 5%. Local rates vary by domicile. Audi FS also lists Louisiana among states where local jurisdictions may bill personal property tax on leased vehicles, added to the lease balance.

Can I buy my leased Audi? Does Audi refinance?

You can buy the leased car at any time (payoff quote in myAudi). If you purchase it you do not pay a turn-in fee, excess wear and use, or excess mileage. AFS does not refinance.

What happens at the end of an Audi lease?

Four paths: turn in toward a new Audi, extend, purchase, or turn in. Turn-in fee can be waived if you lease or buy a new or CPO Audi through AFS within 90 days. Inspections are done only by AiM.

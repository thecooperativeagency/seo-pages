Vehicle service contract

A vehicle service contract is extra repair coverage after the factory warranty. The plan here runs 7 years or 100,000 miles. We would suggest this if you are financing and will keep the car past year four. On a typical short lease you are still inside the factory warranty, so it is usually low priority. Consumer Reports.

Gap protection

If the car is totaled or stolen, gap protection covers the difference between the insurance check and what you still owe. Check your lease contract before adding it. On a loan, it is worth talking about if you put little money down or take a long term. NerdWallet notes that gap coverage from your insurance company often costs less than buying it from a dealer.

Paint and interior protection

Helps with stains, wear, and the finish on paint and interior surfaces. 5 years. Useful if you have kids, pets, or street parking.

Theft protection

Pays a $5,000 benefit if the car is stolen. 5 years.

Windshield protection

Chip and crack repair. A spidered windshield can be a lease-end charge. 5 years.

Tire and wheel, dent, and key replacement

One plan for potholes and road hazards, small dent repair, and a lost or damaged key (up to $800). 5 years. The piece that usually fits a lease is tire and wheel plus key replacement — especially on the roads in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge. Road report, April 2025.

FAQ

Can a dealer make me buy gap coverage or an extended warranty?

No. Federal consumer protection agencies are clear that these products are optional and not required to get a loan.

Do I need gap coverage on an Audi lease?

Check your lease contract for any included gap coverage. On a loan, gap protection is worth talking about if you put little money down or take a long term.

Is tire and wheel protection worth it in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

Here in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge, a national road report from April 2025 found that 60% of the area’s major roads are in poor or mediocre shape, costing drivers about $945 a year extra. The tire and wheel plan covers potholes. Your auto insurance may also cover pothole damage, minus your deductible.

Does car insurance cover flood damage in Louisiana?

Comprehensive insurance covers floods and hurricanes. That’s a question for your insurance, not a dealership product. After a total loss on a loan, gap coverage is what covers the difference between the insurance check and what you still owe.

Is an extended warranty worth it on an Audi?

A typical three-year lease stays inside the factory 4-year/50,000-mile warranty, so a vehicle service contract is usually low priority. It matters more if you’ll keep the car past year four. Consumer Reports found owners often paid more than they got back.

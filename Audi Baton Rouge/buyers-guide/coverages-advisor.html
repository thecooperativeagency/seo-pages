Product cards (Audi Pure Protection, as of Oct 19, 2026)

GAP waiver

After a total loss or theft, waives the difference between insurance and what you still owe. Audi: no maximum on covered losses; up to $1,000 of the deductible. Worked example on Audi’s page: $51k value + $1k deductible vs $55k owed → $5k waived. Not available for purchase on a lease vehicle.

Fit: finance, especially small down / 72–84 months. Lease: check the contract first. Audi GAP. NerdWallet: insurer GAP may cost less than dealer GAP.

Lease-End Protection

Waives up to $10,000 excess wear and use, up to 500 excess miles, missing parts up to $150; no deductible; any single item over $1,000 excluded; VW Credit Leasing only; not in NY.

Lease only. Kids, pets, street parking, potholes, plan to turn it in. Audi LEP.

Tire & wheel

Road hazards including potholes; terms up to 7 years; cosmetic wheel option up to 8 repairs; tow $100. Excludes tread under 3/32 and construction-zone damage; no cosmetic on chrome.

Both lease and finance. Strong in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge: TRIP Apr 9, 2025 — 60% of major roads poor or mediocre; ~$945/yr extra operating cost. Also 20-inch+ wheels. Triple-I: collision can cover potholes (deductible applies). Audi Tire & Wheel.

Dent / PDR

PDR on dents up to 4 inches; hail up to the lesser of your deductible or $1,000 where available.

Strong on leases (Audi: no more than 3 quarter-size dings per panel). Parking lots, hail, outdoors. Multi-Coverage.

Windshield

Chip and crack repair under 6 inches, no limit on repairs; hail excluded; not sold in FL.

Audi turn-in allows only a single chip without spidering. Interstate, construction, gravel. Triple-I: comprehensive covers a broken windshield.

Key replacement

Up to $800 per occurrence; lockout $100; tow $100; transport $75.

Missing key is a lease-end charge (LEP missing parts only up to $150). Multi-driver households, boats, people who lose things.

Vehicle Service Protection

Powertrain, Gold, or Platinum up to 10 yr/120k. EV Platinum up to 8 yr/100k; excludes the high-voltage battery (factory 8/100k on PHEV). Rental $35/day for 10 days; trip interruption $200/day for 5 days; tow $100.

Finance / keep 5+ years. Low value on a lease inside 4/50 unless you will blow 50k. CR: automaker-backed, buy before factory warranty ends. Audi VSP.

Prepaid maintenance / Term Protection

Signature Care included MY2026+ (3 yr/30k). Audi Care (MY2025 and older) and Care Select (30k–120k) optional. Term Protection: wear items up to 6 yr/72k (pads, battery, belts, wipers, alignment, bulbs); buy within 24 mo/24k.

Included plans already cover most of a 36-month lease. Extended plans fit long keep. Warranty page.

Theft protection

Etched code and decals; up to $5,000 benefit; 6 years; not cancelable; not paid if unlocked or keys left in.

Comprehensive already covers theft (Triple-I). This is deductible / benefit. Street or apartment parking.

How to buy smart (neutral box)

CFPB: optional, generally cancelable for unused premium. FTC: cost adds up; prices in writing. Consumer Reports on extended warranties (Jeff Bartlett, Oct 30, 2025): owners often paid more than they got back; prices negotiable; automaker plan; reliability matters. NerdWallet on GAP (Mar 10, 2026): insurer GAP roughly $50–$150/yr vs roughly $500–$700 dealer/lender; rolled-in GAP accrues interest; drop it once you owe less than the car. Flood is comprehensive insurance, not F&I.

Questions

FAQ

Can a dealer make me buy GAP or an extended warranty?

The CFPB says add-ons like extended warranties and GAP are optional and not required to get an auto loan. The FTC says you don’t have to buy add-ons to get financing; ask for prices in writing. Audi Pure Protection products are described by Audi USA as optional and not insurance, and not required to obtain financing.

Do I need GAP on an Audi lease?

Audi’s own GAP product is not available for purchase on a lease vehicle. Check the lease contract for any included gap language. Do not treat an unverified forum claim as fact. On a financed car, GAP is a conversation when down payment is small or the term is long.

Is tire and wheel protection worth it in Louisiana and in Baton Rouge?

TRIP (April 9, 2025) reports 60% of major roads in the Baton Rouge area are in poor or mediocre condition, with about $945 a year in extra vehicle operating costs from rough roads. Audi Tire & Wheel lists potholes among covered road hazards. Collision insurance can also cover pothole damage (Triple-I), with your deductible.

Does car insurance cover flood damage in Louisiana?

Triple-I: comprehensive covers natural disasters such as floods and hurricanes. Flood damage to the car is an insurance question, not an F&I product. After a total loss on a loan, GAP is the product that addresses the gap between insurance and what you still owe.

Is an extended warranty worth it on an Audi?

A typical 36-month lease stays inside Audi’s 4-year/50,000-mile new-vehicle limited warranty, so a vehicle service contract is usually low priority on that lease. It is a finance / long-keep conversation after year four or 50,000 miles. Consumer Reports: survey owners often paid more than they got back; if you buy one, favor the automaker-backed plan.

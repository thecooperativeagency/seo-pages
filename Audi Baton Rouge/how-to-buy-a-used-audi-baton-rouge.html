What should I look for when buying a used car?

Three things: the history, the condition, and the paperwork. History means the vehicle history report. Condition means time on a test drive. Paperwork starts with the federal Buyers Guide sticker in the window.

That Buyers Guide is required on every used car a dealer sells, and it becomes part of your contract. It tells you whether the vehicle is sold with a warranty and what that coverage is. Read it on every used car you consider, here or anywhere else.

Then drive the car like you will actually use it. Highway speed, rough pavement, parking, every button.

What's the difference between used and Certified Pre-Owned?

A used Audi is sold as a pre-owned vehicle from this lot. A Certified Pre-Owned Audi adds a factory program on top: typically 5 years or newer with no more than 60,000 miles, a vehicle history report, a comprehensive inspection covering more than 125 components, and an Audi CPO Limited Warranty.

So the question is the specific car. A three-year-old Audi with low miles may qualify for certification; an eight-year-old one will not, and may still be the right value. Compare CPO inventory with the broader used lot, and read the CPO guide for what certification actually includes.

How do taxes and title work on a used car in Louisiana?

Same as a new car: tax is based on your home parish, not the dealer’s address, and it is paid when the vehicle is titled and registered. Title and registration do not issue until the tax is paid. For in-state sales it is due by the 40th day after the sale. The official table is taxes.mfn.dps.la.gov.

Two useful facts. A trade-in titled in your name (or your spouse’s) reduces taxable price — you are taxed on the difference. And for sales by a licensed Louisiana dealer on or after January 1, 2023, notarization is not required on the MSO, title, or bill of sale. We submit the title and registration file.

FAQ

Does every used car come with a warranty?

Not automatically — and the federal Buyers Guide sticker in the window of every used vehicle tells you exactly what coverage, if any, the vehicle is sold with. That sticker is required by law and becomes part of your sales contract, so read it on any used car you consider. Certified Pre-Owned Audis carry the Audi CPO Limited Warranty; coverage on other used vehicles varies by vehicle.

What's the difference between a used Audi and a Certified Pre-Owned Audi?

Certified Pre-Owned is a factory program with eligibility rules — typically 5 years old or newer, no more than 60,000 miles — plus a vehicle history report, a comprehensive inspection of more than 125 components, and an Audi CPO Limited Warranty. A standard used Audi is a pre-owned vehicle from the dealership lot but doesn't carry the factory CPO warranty.

How is sales tax handled when I buy a used car in Louisiana?

Tax is based on your home parish — not the dealership's address — and is paid when the vehicle is titled and registered. For in-state sales it's due by the 40th day after the sale, and title and registration aren't issued until it's paid. The official Louisiana parish tax table lists the rate for your address.

Can I use my trade-in to lower my taxes on a used car?

Yes. If the trade-in is titled in your name or your spouse's, Louisiana reduces your taxable price by the trade value — you pay tax on the difference. The credit can't exceed the sales price of the vehicle you're buying.

Do I need anything notarized when I buy from a dealer?

For sales by licensed Louisiana dealers on or after January 1, 2023, notarization is not required on the manufacturer's statement of origin, title, or bill of sale. The dealership handles and submits the title and registration paperwork.

Can I finance a used vehicle at Audi Baton Rouge?

Yes. Used vehicles can be financed through the same application at audibatonrouge.com/finance, and the dealership works with lenders to find terms for your situation.

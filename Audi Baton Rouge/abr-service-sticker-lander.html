Audi Maintenance Schedule

When Audi says to come in

Audi recommends maintenance every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. Exact items can vary by VIN and model year.

Minor maintenance

10,000 miles or 1 year

  • Engine oil and filter change
  • Brake pad thickness check
  • Multi-point inspection
  • Service reminder reset

Standard maintenance

20,000 miles or 2 years

  • Everything in the 10k service
  • Broader standard inspection (tires, lights, underbody, fluids, road test)
  • Cabin / dust and pollen filter replacement

Minor maintenance

30,000 miles

  • Minor maintenance service
  • Oil and filter change
  • Brake pad thickness check
  • Multi-point inspection

Standard maintenance

40,000 miles

  • Standard maintenance service
  • Fuller multi-point inspection and road test
  • Model-dependent items may apply (spark plugs, transmission fluid)

Brake fluid: every 2 years, regardless of mileage. Engine air filter: typically every 60,000 miles on most models. Spark plugs and S tronic fluid intervals vary by model. Brakes

Brakes

Pads wear down. If metal hits the rotor, damage gets expensive fast and stopping power drops.

  • Common signs: squeal, grind, longer stops, vibration in the pedal
  • Pad thickness is checked during Audi scheduled maintenance
  • Do not wait for metal-on-metal
Schedule Brake Check

Pad wear

New vs worn

When pad material is gone, the backing plate can score the rotor. That turns a pad job into pads and rotors.

If you hear grind, smell hot brakes, or feel pull/vibration, bring it in. Use the 10% recommended-service offer when it applies.

Tires

Tires

Tread is your contact patch. When it is gone, wet roads and hard stops get worse quickly.

  • Wear bars are molded into the tread. When the tread is even with the bars, you are at about 2/32 inch — replace the tire
  • Penny test: if you can see all of Lincoln’s head, tread is too low
  • Uneven wear often points to alignment or inflation issues
  • Premium options commonly fit Audis: Michelin, Continental, Pirelli, Bridgestone, Goodyear
Book Tire / Alignment Check $100 Off Alignment

Wear bars

How to spot them

Look across the tread for raised bars in the grooves. When the rubber around them wears down to the same height, the tire is at the wear limit.

Do not wait for cords. At 2/32 inch, wet traction falls off hard.

If one tire is wearing faster than the others, ask for an alignment check. ABR currently has $100 off regular (non-ACC) alignment.

Battery

Battery

Modern Audis often use AGM batteries and sensitive charging systems. A weak battery can look like an electrical problem.

  • Have the battery tested at service visits — especially before summer heat and winter cold
  • Short trips, heat, and sitting unused all stress batteries
  • Replacement often needs coding/registration so the car manages charge correctly
Schedule Battery Test

Why Audis are picky

Start-stop and electronics

A weak battery can trigger warnings that look like bigger electrical issues. Testing is faster than guessing.

If the car sits, takes short trips only, or struggles to start, put a battery test on the next visit.

Cabin air filter

Cabin air filter

Outside air enters through the HVAC system. The cabin filter is what catches dust, pollen, and debris before that air hits the cabin.

  • Over time the filter loads up with dirt and can hold odor
  • Symptoms: weak airflow, musty smell, more allergens in the cabin
  • Audi interval: every 20,000 miles or 2 years, whichever comes first
Replace Cabin Filter

What happens over time

Dust, pollen, odor

The filter loads up. Airflow drops. A/C can start to smell. People with allergies notice first.

This is one of the highest-value quick services on an Audi. It is also a factory interval item at 20k / 2 years.

Editor’s note

September feels like a reset, not a slowdown.

Baton Rouge is still hot. School traffic is back. So is Saturday. The week gets tighter and the weekend gets louder.

This issue is for that calendar. The Q5 for city weeks and game-day weekends. The Q7 when more people need to go at once. Service before the season stacks up.

The Q5 for city weeks and game-day weekends.

The Q5 lives in that mix. Sharp enough for dinner. Easy enough for the weekday. Ready for Saturday without making a show of it.

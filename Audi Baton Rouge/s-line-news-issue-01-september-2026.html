On the Road

Next RS6 Avant · Austrian Alps · August 2026

The honest one is still a wagon.

The next RS6 Avant is back on the road after a May barrier at the Nürburgring. Carscoops caught it in the Austrian Alps on 12 August: yellow hybrid labels on the hood, doors, and trunk, 20-spoke wheels under swollen fenders.

The labels prove hybrid. They do not prove cylinder count. The rumor mill will argue V8 against V6; both stories put output around 740 hp. Spring 2027. After fifteen years of wagon-only, a fastback sedan comes with it.

Until then, the point is the same. The Audi you actually want to live with still has a roof you can load.

On the Table

Cocha. Not because it is new.

445 N. 6th, downtown. Chef Saskia Spanhoff. Harvest is the point of the room this month, and Cocha is on the Farm Fête vendor list. The menu already reads like that night: local produce, a light hand, international without trying to be Houston. Patio if the heat breaks. Closed Tuesdays.

Order the Spanish octopus with chorizo Bilbao and white-bean puree. Lamb loin with blackberry Shiraz if you want a second plate. Cachapa if you are sharing. Skip the dry-aged strip unless you came for steak. You did not.

Two people. No laptops. Before Farm Fête, or instead of it.

On the Town

Farm Fête, the 24th.

Thursday, 24 September, 6–9 p.m., Main Street Market. BREADA’s night for the Red Stick Farmers Market: local chefs, farm food, cocktails, two stages, dancing. Dressy casual. Early tickets $95, then $125. Cocha is cooking it.

This is the jacket-on night that is not a concert. Jason Carter is at the Manship the same evening. Pick one.

On the Wrist

Omega Speedmaster 38 mm · ref. 324.30.38.50.01.002

The piece you forget until someone asks.

You do not need another smartwatch. Omega’s new Speedmaster 38 mm, black no-date. Moonwatch face, smaller case, $6,500. Still 14.75 mm thick. Oval subdials are gone. Ignore the diamond bezels.

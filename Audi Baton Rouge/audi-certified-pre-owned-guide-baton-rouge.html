What makes an Audi "Certified Pre-Owned"?

An Audi CPO vehicle is typically 5 years old or newer with no more than 60,000 miles, comes with a vehicle history report, and has passed a comprehensive inspection covering more than 125 components. The program is exclusive to Audi dealers.

That eligibility bar does the first round of filtering: newer cars, lower miles, clean history. The inspection does the second. Browse what is certified now in CPO inventory, and see the store’s CPO program page for the OEM overview.

What warranty comes with a CPO Audi?

The Audi CPO Limited Warranty covers 1 year or 20,000 miles, whichever comes first — and when it starts depends on the car. If factory New Vehicle Limited Warranty remains (4 years/50,000 miles from new), that factory coverage runs first, and CPO coverage begins when it ends. If the factory warranty has already expired, CPO coverage starts the day you buy.

Two clarifications. The CPO warranty is its own coverage — it is not an extension of the factory warranty, and it is not bumper-to-bumper. On e-tron and other electrified models, the high-voltage battery is not covered by the CPO warranty, though it may still be covered under remaining factory warranty. The warranty booklet spells out covered systems and exclusions. We will walk you through it on the specific car.

Where can CPO repairs be done?

Covered CPO repairs are performed free of charge at any authorized Audi dealer in the U.S. Buy the car here, have a covered issue in another city, and that Audi store handles it.

Exclusions exist. Confirm coverage on a specific repair in the booklet or with us before you buy. Our service department is Monday–Friday 7:00am to 6:00pm and Saturday 8:00am to 2:00pm.

Does CPO include roadside assistance?

Yes — 1 year of roadside assistance with unlimited miles, and it attaches to the vehicle. New Audis carry 4 years of roadside from the factory; CPO provides its own year on certified vehicles. If your specific car still has remaining factory coverage, ask us how roadside looks on that VIN. We will not invent the stack.

Can I transfer the CPO warranty if I sell the car?

For CPO vehicles sold on or after August 1, 2023, no — the CPO Limited Warranty is non-transferable and covers the original CPO purchaser only. If resale flexibility matters a lot, factor that in, and compare with buying new.

Is CPO worth it versus a regular used Audi?

You are paying for a factory warranty, a verified history, and an inspection of more than 125 components. Vehicles outside the CPO window — older than 5 years or past 60,000 miles — can still be the right value; shop those with the used buying guide.

FAQ

What qualifies an Audi for Certified Pre-Owned?

Audi CPO vehicles are typically 5 years old or newer with no more than 60,000 miles. Each comes with a vehicle history report and passes a comprehensive inspection covering more than 125 components. The program is exclusive to authorized Audi dealers.

How long is the Audi CPO warranty?

The Audi CPO Limited Warranty runs 1 year or 20,000 miles, whichever comes first. If the vehicle has remaining factory New Vehicle Limited Warranty, the factory coverage runs first and CPO coverage begins when it ends. If the factory warranty has expired, CPO coverage begins at the CPO purchase date.

Is the CPO warranty the same as the factory warranty?

No. The CPO Limited Warranty is separate coverage — it is not an extension of the factory warranty and is not bumper-to-bumper. Coverage details and exclusions are in the warranty booklet, and the dealership can review them with you on a specific vehicle.

Is the high-voltage battery on an electric Audi covered by the CPO warranty?

No — the high-voltage battery is not covered under the CPO Limited Warranty. It may still be covered under the vehicle's remaining factory warranty, so ask the dealership about the coverage picture on the specific vehicle.

Can the next owner use my CPO warranty if I sell the car?

For CPO vehicles sold on or after August 1, 2023, no. The CPO Limited Warranty is non-transferable and applies to the original CPO purchaser only.

Where can I get CPO warranty repairs done?

Covered repairs are performed free of charge at any authorized Audi dealer in the United States — not just the dealership where you bought the vehicle. Exclusions apply; the warranty booklet or your dealer can confirm coverage on a specific repair.

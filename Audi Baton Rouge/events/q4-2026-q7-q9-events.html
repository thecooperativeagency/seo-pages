Top 3 picks

Pick 1

LSU vs. Alabama & LSU vs. Texas

Sat Nov 7 & Sat Nov 14 · Tiger Stadium

The two biggest games of the year bring the largest, most affluent crowd in Baton Rouge. A hosted tailgate with a Q7/Q9 on display is the strongest play we have.

Pick 2

St. Francisville Food & Wine Festival

Nov 13–15 · St. Francisville

An upscale weekend with chef dinners, a jazz brunch, and a Grand Tasting at The Myrtles. The plantation lawns are made for parked luxury SUVs, and no auto sponsor is listed.

Pick 3

Festival of Lights + Cars & Coffee

Sat Dec 5 · Downtown BR + Siegen Lane

Baton Rouge's biggest family holiday event, exactly the three-row buyer. Pair it with Cars & Coffee on Siegen that morning for a nearly free Q9 showing.

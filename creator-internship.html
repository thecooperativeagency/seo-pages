The work

You start with the cash engine: inventory photography and social content for our dealerships. Cars on the lot, shot well, posted fast. It's real volume with a real bar, and it teaches you how the whole machine runs.

But we're a small team, and small teams don't do lanes. If you can write, design, cut video, build email, or think in systems, there's work waiting — rooftop magazines, websites, paid campaigns, other brands entirely. You won't get all of it day one. You'll get as much as you can carry.

Who we want

Someone inspired, hardworking, and a little entrepreneurial. You already shoot, edit, and post — self-taught is fine, your feed is your résumé. We'll hand you real responsibility, not meeting notes and coffee runs. If you're collecting internships for a line on a résumé, this isn't it. If you want your work in the wild, it is.

LSU or the Baton Rouge area is a plus, but the work matters more than the major. College intern setups work here too — credit, flexible scheduling, the whole path — if that's your situation.

Not a typical agency internship.

Most internships teach you how agencies used to work: decks, endless rounds, content that dies in a folder. We built The Cooperative differently — operators first, creative second, systems always.

Operators, not theater

You work on live accounts and real production. Good work gets used. Weak work gets fixed. Portfolio > polish theater.

AI in the workflow

We deeply adapt AI into research, drafting, editing speed, iteration, and internal systems — so you learn how modern teams actually move.

Small team, high ownership

No 40-person ladder. Strong interns get trusted fast. You will feel the difference between “assist” and “own a lane.”

Local brands, real stakes

Dealership and brand work that has to perform in market — not mock campaigns for a classroom grade.

What you’d actually do

Short-form video that feels native

Reels, TikToks, vertical cuts, hooks, captions, pacing. Content people finish watching — not “we posted today” filler.

Platform taste + brand judgment

You already know what scrolls. We’ll teach you how to aim that instinct at real businesses without killing the vibe.

Ship, measure, iterate

Create → publish systems → learn what worked → do it again faster. AI helps the loop. Taste still decides.

This role exists because we need more of the cultural, trend-forward video layer than a traditional shop cadence covers. If you only want Canva statics and meeting notes, this is not it.
